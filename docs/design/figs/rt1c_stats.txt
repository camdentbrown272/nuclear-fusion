== 4. D-specific cosmic-neutron backgrounds absent from the H2O twin (order of magnitude)
   D atoms within product range of the telescope: membrane 2.9e+20, electrolyte film (100 um) 2.1e+21, front gas 3.4e+19; total 2.4e+21
   d(n,np) breakup [low]: 0.017 breakups/day/cell; x acceptance*PID-window fraction 0.03 -> 0.0005 protons/day/cell in the T1 window
   d(n,np) breakup [high]: 0.167 breakups/day/cell; x acceptance*PID-window fraction 0.08 -> 0.0133 protons/day/cell in the T1 window
   n-d elastic recoil d [low]: 1.04/day/cell; reaching telescope with E>1.4 MeV x0.05 = 0.052/day; p/d PID leakage 0.001 -> 0.00005/day in T1 window
   n-d elastic recoil d [high]: 7.82/day/cell; reaching telescope with E>1.4 MeV x0.15 = 1.173/day; p/d PID leakage 0.01 -> 0.01173/day in T1 window
   H2O twin: n-p recoil protons 0.006/day (M5) -> the D-H difference is NOT signed-conservative:
   D-specific terms of 1e-3 to 3e-2/day are comparable to the whole modelled background.
   They are flux-independent: a within-cell D flux-on vs D flux-off (static-loaded) contrast
   cancels them; the D-vs-H contrast does not.
