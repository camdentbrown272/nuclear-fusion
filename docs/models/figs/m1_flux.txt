M1 Part D - deuterium flux as a variable
  Bulk: J=1e+14 D/cm2/s -> drift/hop-flux ratio J*a/(n D) = 2.3e-10: flux does not change bulk site occupancies (O, T, vacancy, dislocation) measurably.
  Bulk: J=1e+16 D/cm2/s -> drift/hop-flux ratio J*a/(n D) = 2.3e-08: flux does not change bulk site occupancies (O, T, vacancy, dislocation) measurably.
  So any flux effect must live at the exit face, entry face or buried interfaces, where J sets coverage and the rate of recombination encounters.

  Exit-face kinetics (Pd, vacuum side). J_max = recombination-limited flux (theta=1):
   T=300 K: J_max = 3.6e+13 .. 1.7e+15 D/cm2/s
   T=350 K: J_max = 5.1e+15 .. 1.4e+17 D/cm2/s
   T=400 K: J_max = 2.1e+17 .. 3.9e+18 D/cm2/s
   T=500 K: J_max = 4.0e+19 .. 4.0e+20 D/cm2/s
  -> at room temperature a bare Pd exit face into vacuum saturates at ~4e+13-2e+15 D/cm2/s; above that D piles up subsurface and the permeation flux is recombination-limited.
     To run flux as a variable up to 1e16 D/cm2/s the exit face must be warm (>=350-400 K) or carry a recombination catalyst - but a Pt/Pd-black catalyst layer changes the very site population being tested. PdO also blocks recombination (lower J_max) - design conflict flagged for M3/M6/M7.
  Energetics: on Pd, 2 D(ads) -> D2(g) is ENDOTHERMIC by ~0.9 eV (adsorption heat, Christmann 1988) and D(bulk) -> 1/2 D2(g) is endothermic by ~0.2 eV/D; the 0.5-1 eV 'released per molecule' in the brief applies only to gas-phase atom recombination. Nascent D2 leaves near-thermal: no hot pairs.

  Static comparison: the exit face permanently holds 3*1.5e15 = 4.5e15 NN surface pairs/cm2.
  Flux-driven transition-state pair-time per cm2 = (J/2)*tau_TS = 50 pair-s per s at J=1e16: flux adds the equivalent of ~50 permanent molecular-distance pairs per cm2.

  Fusion probability per recombination encounter p = lambda_mol(U_eff) * tau_TS (rho0 of D2):
   U_eff=  34 eV (D2-molecule (standard)  ): p = 2.6e-78
   U_eff= 127 eV (accel. low, Pd          ): p = 1.3e-42
   U_eff= 209 eV (accel. high, Pd surface ): p = 3.5e-34
   U_eff= 247 eV (accel. PdO              ): p = 8.6e-32
  C3 Si channel: s_min = 66 counts/30 d, eps(surface) = 0.270 -> R_min = 9.5e-05 fusions/s
   T=300 K, J=1e+12: 1.0e+12 encounters/s -> p_min = 9.5e-17 per encounter (x4e+61 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 1211 eV)
   T=300 K, J=1e+14: 1.0e+14 encounters/s -> p_min = 9.5e-19 per encounter (x4e+59 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 898 eV)
   T=300 K, J=1e+16: 2.5e+14 encounters/s -> p_min = 3.9e-19 per encounter (x1e+59 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 851 eV)
   T=450 K, J=1e+12: 1.0e+12 encounters/s -> p_min = 9.5e-17 per encounter (x4e+61 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 1211 eV)
   T=450 K, J=1e+14: 1.0e+14 encounters/s -> p_min = 9.5e-19 per encounter (x4e+59 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 898 eV)
   T=450 K, J=1e+16: 1.0e+16 encounters/s -> p_min = 9.5e-21 per encounter (x4e+57 over D2-standard, U_eff-equiv 692 eV)
  -> a flux-triggered anomaly is detectable only if a recombination encounter fuses with p >~ 1e-18..-16; static D2-molecule physics gives 3e-78. That is the M7 lock-in target.

  Flux modulation (M7): with square-wave on/off at 50 % duty, signal S appears only in 'on' bins; background (radon, cosmic, detector) is common-mode. Statistics are those of on-minus-off counting: s_min rises by sqrt(2) (~1.4x) vs a known-background count, but slow background systematics (barometric +-14 %, radon plate-out) cancel to first order if the period << their timescale (hours): choose 1-10 min periods (membrane diffusion time L^2/D = 31 s for 25 um sets the upper frequency ~1/(2*that)).
