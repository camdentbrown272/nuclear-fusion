2. SELECTION RULES FOR A TWO-LASER BEAT
  Rocksalt (Fm-3m): zone-centre optical mode is T1u -> IR-active, first-order Raman-INACTIVE (as in NaCl).
  A difference-frequency (stimulated-Raman / impulsive) drive therefore cannot couple to the bulk G-mode
  at first order; coupling exists only where inversion symmetry is broken (surface layer, defects).
  Beat wavevector (co-propagating 670/682.7 nm in D2O): 2.32e+05 m^-1; counter-propagating max ~2.47e+07 m^-1;
  L-point wavevector |q_L| = 1.35e+10 m^-1 -> photons supply <1e-3 of the momentum. L-point (zone-boundary)
  modes can be driven only through atomic-scale disorder; Hagelstein's L-point assignment is kinematically
  disfavoured for any optical drive.
