Source location: heater on wall vs excess in bed changes nothing in the lumped network
(both leave through the wall), but the bed-to-wall gas conductance sets the powder temperature:
  G2 50 W heater in bed, D2 (k=0.14 W/mK)  : T_bed - T_wall =  100.0 K
  G2 50 W heater in bed, H2 (k=0.18 W/mK)  : T_bed - T_wall =   75.4 K
  G2 50 W heater in bed, vacuum            : T_bed - T_wall = 1000.0 K
  -> the D2 run and the H2 control run have different bed temperatures at equal heater power;
     a thermally-activated artefact (e.g. desorption/absorption enthalpy) is NOT cancelled by the control.
