B1. Fracto-emission / crack-tip charge separation
    Physics limits: in a metal (PdD, TiD2) crack-face charge relaxes in ~eps0/sigma_el ~ 1e-18 s; the only sustained gap voltage is the contact-potential difference (<~1 V) [BK].
    Oxide-skinned faces (PdO 2-10 nm, TiO2 2-5 nm): V <= E_bd*t_ox ~ 1e9 V/m x 1e-8 m = 10 V [BK, thin-oxide breakdown].
    Ionic insulator (LiD): surface fields 1e7-1e9 V/m (brief/literature), vacuum gap (no D2 gas), V grows with opening w until field emission/flashover; w_max = 1-10 um [guess].
    D2 in PdD cracks at x~0.9: fugacity 1e3-1e4 bar -> n ~ 2e22 cm^-3, mean free path 0.5 nm -> collisional; ions gain E*lambda only.

B2. Desorption / phase-transition transients (Lipson Pd/PdO:Dx)
    PdO/Pd Schottky built-in potential                  : <= 0.80 eV -> x1.3 at U_eff=127 eV, x3.1e+04 at U_eff=11 eV (TF)
    D+D -> D2 recombination energy (brief: 0.5-1 eV)    : <= 1.00 eV -> x1.4 at U_eff=127 eV, x3.4e+05 at U_eff=11 eV (TF)
    Heyrovsky step at 1 V overpotential                 : <= 1.00 eV -> x1.4 at U_eff=127 eV, x3.4e+05 at U_eff=11 eV (TF)
    beta->alpha transition strain energy per D          : <= 0.05 eV -> x1 at U_eff=127 eV, x2 at U_eff=11 eV (TF)
    On Pd the recombinative desorption is endothermic (adsorption heat ~0.9 eV/D2, Christmann 1988 [BK]), so no hot D is produced; the 1 eV entries are upper bounds.
    Lipson-scale claim 0.1 p/s/cm^2 from the interface layer (7.8e+15 pairs/cm^2) needs lambda = 2.6e-17 /pair/s -> static U_eff >= 286 eV
    Lipson-scale claim 0.1 p/s/cm^2 from the top 1 um (3.7e+19 pairs/cm^2) needs lambda = 5.5e-21 /pair/s -> static U_eff >= 218 eV
    Static accelerator-PdO screening mapped to thermal (U_eff 247 eV, m1_sites) predicts 2.6e-03 fusions/s/cm^2 = 1.3e-03 p/s/cm^2 at the interface: 78x below the claim, but inside C3's reach (see Part C). Transients add <x1.5.

B6. Czerski 0+ threshold resonance: S-factor multiplier K(E)
    Breit-Wigner with Gamma_d(E) = 2 theta^2 gamma_W^2 (2 pi R/a) e^{4 sqrt(2R/a)} e^{-2 pi eta(E)}:
    for E << E_R the Gamow factors cancel and K = 1 + C/((E-E_R)^2 + Gamma^2/4) is energy independent.
    Unitarity-style ceiling (theta^2=1, R=5 fm, Gamma_out=0.44 eV): C_max = 3.33e+07 eV^2
    Calibration: the resonance must supply the excess at E_cm=2.5 keV between U_e=340 eV and the resonance-fit U_e=105 eV (Szczecin): x2.54.

    -> If E_R sits within ~1 eV of threshold, K(thermal) ~ 2e+08 (= +19 e-folds). If E_R is a few keV above threshold, K(thermal) ~ 1.2-1.4. The data do not fix E_R.
    Even K=2e8 raises the D2-molecule rate only from 3e-64 to ~6e-56 /s: the resonance changes WHAT to detect, not WHETHER cold D-D is detectable (confirms R3 Table D).
    Products if real: Gamma_ee/Gamma_tot ~ 10/11 -> per fusion 0.91 e+e- pair (E_e+ + E_e- = 22.8 MeV, continuum), 2 x 511 keV back-to-back after e+ stops, bremsstrahlung up to ~20 MeV; p and n branches each ~4.5%. A neutron-only or p-only detector under-counts by x11; a 511-511 coincidence pair plus a >3 MeV gamma/electron window is needed.

B7. Thermal-spike 'plateau' (UC Davis/LBNL 2026; Szczecin 2024-26) - not available beam-free
    Plateau yield per incident D taken = thick-target yield at E_d=2.5 keV with U_e=340 eV (ZrD2): 7.9e-17 fusions/D
    To reach the C3 proton threshold (2.7e-3 /s) a device needs 3.4e+13 keV deuterons/s = 5.5 uA of keV D+ -> an ion beam. Cold sources of keV deuterons:
      cosmic knock-on recoils in 1 cm3 PdD: 5e-04 /s (B5); LiD fracture at 1e9 V/m: ~5e13 D+ per cm2 once per fresh face (B1). Neither gives a sustained 1e14 /s.
    A cold drive (current pulse, laser <=1 W) raises local T by <=1e2 K; a thermal spike needs ~keV into ~nm^3 (T ~ 1e4 K). Equivalent spike rate from cold means: 0.
    Conclusion: the plateau mechanism contributes <= 8e-20 fusions/s in a beam-free device.
